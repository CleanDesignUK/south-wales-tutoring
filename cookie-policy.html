Privacy and choices

Cookie policy

Understand the website’s cookie and local storage use, including how to change your preferences.

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This notice applies to the South Wales Tutoring website.

Essential website storage

We store a simple preference called swt-cookie-consent-v1 in your browser's local storage so the cookie banner does not appear on every visit. The value is either accepted or declined. It does not contain your identity.

Analytics and advertising

In this website release, we have not installed optional Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or advertising cookies. Choosing Accept or Decline currently changes only your saved preference; no optional tracking is enabled by either choice.

Third-party resources

Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons and the Manrope font may be loaded from their CDN or font-provider domains. Their respective providers may receive normal technical request data. The enquiry form connects to Web3Forms only when you submit the form.

How to change your choice

Use the control or the Cookie settings link in the footer. You can also clear this site's local storage in your browser settings.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, email southwales@nwttutoring.com or see the privacy policy.
