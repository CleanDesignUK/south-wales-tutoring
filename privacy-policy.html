Privacy policy

How South Wales Tutoring handles the personal information you give us through our website and tutoring enquiries.

Last updated: 11 November 2026. South Wales Tutoring operates as part of North Wales Tutoring Group. This policy describes our handling of personal information through this website. It should be read alongside the group’s wider service terms where applicable.

1. Who to contact

For privacy questions or requests to access, update or delete information, email southwales@nwttutoring.com. South Wales Tutoring is part of North Wales Tutoring Group.

2. Information collected

When you enquire, we may receive your name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, message (which may contain the student’s subject, school stage and general area), and any information you choose to include in it. We may also receive ordinary technical data when you use the website and form services.

3. Why we use it

We use your details to respond to an enquiry, find an appropriate tutor, arrange requested lessons, manage our tutoring relationship and meet applicable legal responsibilities. We do not require consent to marketing in order to submit an enquiry.

4. Our lawful bases

Depending on the activity, processing may rely on taking steps before entering a tutoring contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and operating the service. We assess the appropriate lawful basis for the activity.

5. Form provider and other services

Our enquiry forms are submitted using Web3Forms, a third-party email form processor. Information submitted may be processed outside the UK, including in the United States. We also use Bootstrap and fonts from external providers for site presentation. Do not submit sensitive personal data through the initial enquiry form.

6. Sharing details

Where necessary to respond or arrange requested tutoring, appropriate details may be shared with relevant staff, contractors or tutors in the wider group. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if legally required.

7. Retention and security

We aim to retain enquiry information only as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to legal, accounting or safeguarding obligations. Security measures and service providers are reviewed in line with our operational responsibilities.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or object to certain processing. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Cookies and storage

Our cookie banner remembers your choice in local storage. No optional advertising or analytics tracking script is installed in this website release. See our cookie policy for details.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice as our services or providers change. The update date at the top of the page will reflect revisions.

Before launch: the organisation should review this notice against its actual internal retention periods, lawful-basis records and tutoring privacy procedures.

Safeguarding concerns

If you need to report a concern about the safety or welfare of a learner, please use our separate safeguarding process rather than the ordinary tutoring enquiry form.
