Made-for-Wales GCSEs: ask about the exact course

New GCSE qualifications have been introduced in stages. For example, Mathematics and Numeracy (Double Award) was introduced for teaching from September 2025, while The Sciences (Double Award) became available from September 2026. Some legacy and separate science qualifications still apply to specific students. We therefore ask for the year group, exam board and qualification name rather than assuming all students sit the same papers.
