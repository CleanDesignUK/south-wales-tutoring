How to Choose an Online Tutor in Cardiff, Swansea or Newport

A practical checklist for South Wales parents choosing a GCSE or A-Level online tutor in Maths, Physics, English or Science.

South Wales Tutoring  ·  11 November 2026  ·  5 min read

In this guide

  1. Check the qualification
  2. Describe the challenge
  3. Ask about teaching
  4. Confirm fees
  5. Think beyond location

When looking for a tutor in Cardiff, Swansea or Newport, it is easy to start by searching for the nearest teacher. For online tutoring, though, location is only one part of the decision. The best fit will depend on the subject, exam board, learning needs, lesson times and how clearly the tutor explains ideas.

1. Start with the subject and qualification

“GCSE Science” covers several possible qualifications. “Maths” might refer to KS3 numeracy, WJEC GCSE Mathematics and Numeracy, GCSE resits or A-Level Maths. Be precise when you contact a provider.

For Wales, tell the provider whether the learner is studying WJEC and which specific course they are taking. New Made-for-Wales GCSEs have been phased in, so the student's year group matters.

2. Describe the actual difficulty

Instead of only saying “needs to improve Maths”, try “understands equations in class but cannot solve unfamiliar algebra questions independently”. That gives a tutor something useful to work with. Other examples include losing marks in Physics due to unit conversions, finding it hard to write English analysis or struggling to remember Biology processes.

3. Ask how the tutor teaches online

A live lesson should be more than watching someone else answer questions. Ask whether the student will explain their thinking, receive feedback and practise during the session. A suitable tutor should be able to adapt when a concept is not understood.

Students who feel anxious about being wrong may benefit from a supportive, calm teaching style with time to think before moving on.

4. Confirm cost and arrangements

Ask the provider for the hourly rate, how long a session lasts, the payment terms, lesson cancellation rules and what happens if a tutor is unavailable. Make sure those details are clear before the first booking.

At South Wales Tutoring, GCSE and KS3 lessons start from £23/hran hour and A-Level lessons start from £28/hran hour, depending on the tutor.

5. Consider online convenience across South Wales

Families in Cardiff, Swansea and Newport can access a wider range of specialisms online without driving to a teaching centre. This can make it easier to find support for A-Level Physics or a particular GCSE specification.
