Helpful answers about online tutoring in South Wales.

Yes. South Wales Tutoring offers live one-to-one online lessons to families in South Wales and throughout South Wales. You can request Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English and other subjects, including WJEC and other UK exam boards, subject to tutor availability.

We offer live online one-to-one tuition focused on tutor matching and experienced UK tutors. Lessons can work through knowledge gaps, assignments, WJEC or other UK exam-board specifications, topic questions, mock exams and revision strategies. Students in South Wales can learn from home without travel. GCSE lessons start at £23/hr and A-Level at £28/hr, with the individual tutor rate confirmed before booking.

We are part of North Wales Tutoring Group. Instead of choosing a stranger from an unverified listing, you can tell our team the student’s year group, topics, exam board and preferred times. We help identify a suitable experienced tutor and explain the available lessons and rate before booking.

Welsh-medium GCSE Maths tutoring is available through relevant tutors in our group, subject to matching and availability. Please mention Welsh-medium teaching and your exact WJEC qualification in your message.
