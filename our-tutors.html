The right match

How we recommend a tutor

Subject knowledge is essential, but it is only one part of a good tutoring match.

  • Subject and level: GCSE Maths needs a different approach from A-Level Physics.
  • Exam specification: WJEC and other boards require relevant materials.
  • Learning needs: confidence, pace, foundational gaps and exam skills.
  • Availability: a lesson time that works around school and family commitments.
